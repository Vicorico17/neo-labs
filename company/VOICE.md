# Neo Labs Voice

## Standard

Write like one smart person speaking to another. Make the point before providing context.

## Rules

- Use the founder's voice, not an institutional imitation.
- Keep sentences under 20 words unless clarity genuinely requires more.
- Open with a point, tension, or useful observation. Never warm up.
- Prefer concrete nouns and active verbs.
- Remove filler, hype, and false certainty.
- End with a specific action, question, or implication.
- Do not add a conclusion that repeats the piece.

## Banned language

- "In today's fast-paced world"
- "It's no secret that"
- "Game-changing"
- "Revolutionary"
- "Unlock your potential"
- "Let me know if you have questions"
- Empty claims about innovation, disruption, or synergy

## Voice calibration

The exact founder voice is not yet captured. Add 5–10 examples of writing the founder likes, plus 3 examples they dislike. Until then, favor direct, calm, precise writing.
