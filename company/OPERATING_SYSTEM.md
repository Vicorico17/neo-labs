# ro.fun Operating System

## Fund workflow

Investment pipeline: Sourced → Screening → Diligence → IC review → Approved → Invested. Watch and Passed preserve decisions that do not proceed. Stage changes alone do not create portfolio holdings or evidence of deployment.

Investor relationships: Identified → Conversation → Diligence → Indication → Committed → Closed. Indications are not commitments. Confirm commitments and closes against source documents.

Internal operations remain separate from recorded portfolio investments. Portfolio counts exclude internal work. Unknown metrics are `TBD`, never zero capital or invented performance.

## Portfolio stages

1. **Idea** — problem and founder insight captured.
2. **Validation** — customer evidence and a falsifiable test underway.
3. **Build** — product and go-to-market milestone defined.
4. **Launch** — real users, distribution, and measurement active.
5. **Scale** — repeatable economics and an explicit growth constraint.
6. **Hold / Exit** — deliberate maintenance, sale, shutdown, or return of capital.

## Weekly cadence

### Monday: portfolio review

- Review every active project.
- Confirm owner, health, milestone, and next action.
- Select the three company-level priorities.
- Stop or defer work that does not support them.

### Wednesday: constraint review

- Identify the largest blocked outcome.
- Assign one decision owner.
- Commission research only when it feeds that decision.

### Friday: evidence and decisions

- Record shipped work, new evidence, and changed assumptions.
- Update pipeline and cash implications.
- Write important decisions to the source of truth.

## Status rules

- **On track** — milestone is likely without intervention.
- **At risk** — milestone needs a decision or additional resource.
- **Blocked** — progress cannot continue until a named dependency changes.
- **Paused** — intentionally receives no resources.

## Required fields

Every project needs one owner, one stage, one health status, one current milestone, one next action, and a due date. Every task needs an owner, project, priority, and due date. Every deal needs a stage, value or check range, next action, and next-action date.

## Agent handoffs

- Operator defines the decision and desired output.
- Researcher supplies verified evidence.
- Writer turns approved thinking into external content.
- Closer turns qualified commercial intent into a clear commitment.
- Operator records the resulting decision, owner, and next action.
