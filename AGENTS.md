# Neo Labs Company OS

Treat this repository as Neo Labs' operating memory. Read the relevant files in `company/` before producing work. Never invent portfolio facts, client facts, financial figures, or investment terms. Mark missing information as `TBD`.

## Route work

- Research or verification: use `$neo-researcher`.
- External writing: use `$neo-writer`.
- Proposals, objections, and sales follow-up: use `$neo-closer`.
- Portfolio status, priorities, owners, and execution: use `$neo-operator`.
- Mixed work: let the Operator define the outcome and hand off each specialist task explicitly.

## Source of truth

- Company model and positioning: `company/COMPANY_OS.md`
- Voice: `company/VOICE.md`
- Investment model: `company/INVESTMENT_THESIS.md`
- Operating cadence: `company/OPERATING_SYSTEM.md`
- Live dashboard state: `data/state.json`
- Role workspaces: `researcher/`, `writer/`, `closer/`, and `operator/`

Update the source of truth when a decision changes. Do not leave important decisions only in chat.

Store reusable briefs and working outputs in the matching role directory. Use descriptive, dated filenames for outputs, such as `researcher/outputs/2026-08-09-market-map.md`.

## Approval boundaries

Draft freely. Do not publish, send, spend, promise terms, contact a prospect, or change a live external system without explicit approval. Investment activity must remain clearly separated from general company operations and reviewed by qualified legal and tax advisers before money is raised or deployed.
